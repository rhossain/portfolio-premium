## Capture notes

These are native screenshots of the live interface, text, photography and spatial floor plan. Fonts and visible images were loaded before capture. The welcome tour and demo banner were dismissed through the site controls. Themes were selected using desktop preferences or the mobile navigation menu. The homepage orbit was paused before capture.

Desktop views include the homepage in both themes, estate search, property intelligence and photography, the spatial floor plan, finance tools, buyer watchlist, comparison, portfolio forecaster, advisor listing and lead views, and governance overview. The portfolio image is positioned to show complete estate cards. The estate gallery uses the Overview tab with the intelligence summary collapsed. Finance and forecaster controls retain their default sample values.

Several views use tabs or modals without a dedicated URL. The manifest records the selected state as well as the source URL. Property views use the estate deep link where available.

The map and staging-slider candidates were excluded from the final gallery because their rendered views were visually incomplete. No UI was redrawn or fabricated to fill those views.

All financial projections, valuations, operational statistics and customer records shown are demo content from the site. Their appearance in these screenshots does not verify the claims. Capture interactions did not submit bids, payments, enquiries, listings or other business records.

The mobile compositions preserve the native mobile screenshots in simple charcoal frames with subtle gold accents. The ZIP includes images/, mobile/, this README, manifest.json and contact-sheet.jpg. The contact sheet is a review aid. No portfolio content entry, hosted storage or deployment was changed.
